## How it works (definitions)
- FINISHED = status completed OR approved hours (clock_in+clock_out, status approved) >= required_hours (>0). Same everywhere. ACTIVE = display_status active. PENDING ACCOUNT = pending_activation (own tile, not active/average). on_list = non-inactive and set up. AVERAGE HOURS = mean hours_progress_pct of on_list interns (finished at real %).
- Finished/pending-account interns never get no_clock_in/behind_hours; Dean no_supervisor and scorecard skip finished; evaluation_due stays.
- BELL BADGE RULE: badge = new notifications (unread and created after users.notifications_seen_at, as before) + number of attention ITEMS with info=false (one per item, not the sum of counts). Opening the bell clears the first part only; the second part stays while the backlog exists. Stored 'attendance_red_flag' notifications (the event) and the live red_flags item (the backlog of unchecked flags) both appear; the item says so in its detail. Nothing is stored for attention items (no duplicates, no daily rows).
- hrefs: supervisor red_flags (/supervisor/attendance?flag=unchecked), auto_closed, tasks_to_rate, ready_to_evaluate (/supervisor/evaluations/new?intern=<dep>), activities (?status=pending); coordinator attention (/coordinator/interns?filter=needs_attention), companies_no_supervisor, pending_accounts, supervisor_invites, documents, finished (?filter=completed); intern clock_in/clock_out/.../tasks_overdue; super admin payments, expired, plan_changes, expiring, inquiries, tickets; Dean billing/coordinator/interns?filter=attention.
